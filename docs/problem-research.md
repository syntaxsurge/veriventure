# Real-World Problem Research

Each challenge targeted by VeriVenture is grounded in evidence from government, multilateral, or academic sources. The table below summarizes the signal and the product response.

| Challenge | Evidence | Why it matters | VeriVenture response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SME financing gap | World Bank estimates a \$5.2T credit shortfall for 65M MSMEs worldwide ([MSME Finance Gap](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/smefinance/publication/msme-finance-gap)). | Entrepreneurs without standardized proof struggle to unlock loans or grants. | Wallet-signed `AchievementBadge` hashes + verification pages give lenders tamper-evident traction proof that maps directly to Hackathon #1’s “AI for Sustainable Business Models” deliverable. |
| Cyber resilience for small firms | CISA warns SMEs remain prime cyber targets and need verifiable controls ([Cyber Essentials](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/services/cyber-essentials)). | Without trustworthy identity, AI copilots can leak or fabricate data. | Wallet-only auth + deterministic document checksums keep every AI artifact traceable, satisfying Hackathon #1’s “Cybersecurity & Data Protection” and Hackathon #2’s “User-centric Apps” focus. |
| Climate and sustainability pressure | IPCC AR6 shows SMEs are disproportionately exposed to climate disruptions without transparent impact reporting ([IPCC WGII Report](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/)). | Governments and corporates demand resilient, provable ESG action. | Milestone hashes + DKG Community Notes let founders publish climate claims with citations, addressing Hackathon #1’s “AI in Climate & Environmental Innovation.” |
| AI readiness & skills gap | Harvard Business Review notes most small businesses lack AI literacy despite demand for automation ([HBR: “How Small Businesses Can Benefit from AI”](https://hbr.org/2023/06/how-small-businesses-can-benefit-from-ai)). | Founders need turnkey copilots tied to their own data, not generic templates. | The AI Assistant (pitch decks, plans, resumes, social autoposting) reuses the same wallet-linked context and stores results in Convex for instant reuse. |
| Trust/alignment for AI agents | NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework stresses provenance + transparency for trustworthy AI ([NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)). | Judges in Hackathon #3 require demonstrable alignment signals. | Truth Alignment Lab compares Grokipedia vs Wikipedia, quantifies similarity via OpenAI embeddings, and publishes JSON-LD Knowledge Assets on the OriginTrail DKG for agent-ready provenance. |

These references are mirrored in the README so contributors can trace requirements back to the original research while ensuring the implementation directly addresses each documented pain point.
